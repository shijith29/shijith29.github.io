RyDoc Shelf

AI-powered study and lesson-planning companion for teachers and students.

Look up any topic from Primary to Postgraduate and get a grade-appropriate summary, then turn it into quizzes, a Bloom's Taxonomy question bank, a lesson plan or a full study book. Everything works in English or Malayalam, with no login and no personal data collected.

  • Book Builder with chapter plans and study hours
  • Lesson Plan Builder with PDF export
  • Bloom's Taxonomy question bank, all six levels
  • Timed practice exams with topic-wise score reports
  • Syllabus Mapper with camera OCR
  • Flashcards and offline access
  • Progress tracking and weak-topic revision
  • Class attendance and notes for teachers

Padanam PSC പഠനം

Offline exam preparation for Kerala PSC and banking exams.

A deliberately different design from RyDoc Shelf: no accounts and no backend. The full question bank lives on the phone in SQLite, so practice works anywhere, on any connection. The app's only network call is an optional check of a static manifest for new content packs.

  • Kerala PSC and banking exam tracks
  • Fully offline question bank on device
  • Malayalam and English interface
  • Downloadable content packs
  • No sign-up, no personal data
  • Part of the RyDoc family
